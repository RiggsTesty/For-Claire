For Claire

Claire,

You are the most beautiful woman in the world — inside and out.

I didn’t just fall in love with you.
I recognized you.
The most hardworking person I know.

The most genuine human being I have ever met.

Real. Kind. Pure.
Dear My Claire,

Happy Valentine’s Day, my love. It may be just another day for others, but to me, it’s a reminder to celebrate you and the love we share. A day to recognize how sweet, beautiful, smart, hardworking, and genuinely amazing you are.

Every day, you give your best. Every day, you show your passion in everything you do. I know that these past two weeks have been hard—life has been heavy, work has been stressful, and you’ve had so much on your plate. But I want you to know that I am always here for you. Patiently waiting for you to open up whenever you’re ready. No pressure my Love, just my sincere respect for you.

That’s why I chose those flowers for you. The bouquet is called “Solace Pink.” It carries shades of yellow, white, pink, and a soft hint of blue. I picked it for a reason. Yellow symbolizes positivity, happiness, and joy. White stands for purity and respect. Blue represents peace, calmness, and trust. And pink reflects gentleness and sweetness—just like you. I didn’t want to give you just any flowers. I wanted to give you something with meaning and something you really need and deserve to have right now.

Everything I do is for you. For us. For our future. I hope you always remember that.

My love, I respect you deeply—for your dreams, your goals, and everything you want to accomplish in life. I support you in everything you love, everything you want to experience, and everything you choose to pursue. All that truly matters to me is your happiness and the peace you deserve.

And once again, I just want to tell you that I love you with all my heart. For a lifetime.

Happy Valentine’s Day, my love.

Forever yours,
Riggs
